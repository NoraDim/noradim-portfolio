A concept with no operating structure yet

[Client] set out to connect a Nigerian workforce to roles on a large-scale infrastructure initiative, [Flagship Infrastructure Project] — spanning renewables, Web3 systems, and advanced construction — using a token-gated community model. The idea existed. The org chart, the training pipeline, the referral economy, and the campaign sequencing did not.

The gap

No documented reporting lines, no defined path from "new recruit" to "trained, revenue-generating contributor," and no shared plan for how outreach, training, and investment messaging should sequence over time.

The brief

Translate a founder's vision into an operable structure: who reports to whom, how a person moves from interest to onboarded contributor, what they're trained on, and how the wider campaign builds trust in phases rather than all at once.

Organizational structure & symbol key

Built the full organogram from founder down through strategist, sales, and media functions — with a symbol key distinguishing established headcount, contract staff, active vacancies, and the Partnership Liaison Officers tier, plus a dashed line marking where the animation role specifically (not the wider media unit) reports externally rather than in-house — so the founder could see capacity gaps at a glance.

A nine-tier contributor progression system

Designed a cadre/rank system so every Partnership Liaison Officer has a visible path forward — entry criteria, referral thresholds, monthly pay bands, and unlocked incentives at each level. Tiers 1 through 4 are each split into two stages, so the full ladder runs nine steps deep: one entry stage plus two stages within each of the four tiers.

Recruitment, onboarding & referral workflows

Mapped the full journey from first contact to active, trained contributor across three teams — an eleven-step flow that removed ambiguity about who owns each handoff.

01

Recruit → Support → Core Team → Academy

A recruit is registered, oriented on vision and structure by the Support Team, trained on foundational concepts by the Core Team, then routed to the Academy for hands-on platform training before returning to Support for final integration into the active pool.

11-step flowchartclear handoff ownershipregistration → active pool
02

Referral flow, separated from recruitment

Referrals (as distinct from direct recruits) follow a shorter three-step path — captured, registered, and guided on platform interaction — so referral volume could be tracked independently of new-member onboarding.

Partnership Liaison role & training academy

Defined a new contract role from scratch — the Partnership Liaison Officer — including its learning pathway, curriculum, and the advisory role (this engagement) accountable for the surrounding operational systems.

Learning pathway

13-step structured progression

From internal culture (litepaper, community-builder training) through external skills (strategic communication, negotiation) to market analysis (tokenomics, asset classification), with digital badges gating advancement.

Role scope (this engagement)

Strategic Technical Advisor

Owned structural strategy for the Academy and PLO program, operational vetting of partner-sourced projects, risk mitigation for outreach activity, and workforce-curriculum alignment.

Governance tools applied

RACI + 4-quadrant prioritization

Used a RACI model to eliminate role confusion across the growing team, and an urgent/important matrix to keep the working roadmap focused on high-impact tasks.

2026 targets set

100 liaison officers · 100,000 contributors

Authored the year-one operational targets: 100 qualified Partnership Liaison Officers and 100,000 Community Builders by 2026.

Campaign strategy: pillars into phases

Contributed to translating the platform's four messaging pillars — workforce, infrastructure, empowerment, and investment trust — into a three-phase go-to-market sequence, so outreach content always had a clear "why now."

Phase 1 — Awareness

Introduce the problem & solution

Establishes the job-engine narrative and institutional trust to create early excitement.

Phase 2 — Engagement

Educate & engage the community

Surfaces training programs and project blueprints to demonstrate tangible, ongoing work.

Phase 3 — Action

Mobilize investment & labor

Invites the community to invest through trusted channels and apply for live roles.

Partner & community outreach mapping

Built a working map of prospective community partners and voices for cross-promotion — tracking audience reach, existing relationships between communities, and a recommended approach for each, so outreach wasn't left to guesswork.

How this was approached

Structure before scale

Every system — org chart, progression tiers, onboarding flow — was documented before headcount grew, so growth didn't outpace clarity on who does what.

Incentives designed alongside process

Reward structures (commission, badges, ambassador status) were built into the same documents as the workflows they motivate, not bolted on afterward.
