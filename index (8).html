Using Ruptvate

These terms cover the Ruptvate website, the forms on it, and the Commander and Scouter programme. By using them you agree to these terms. We may update them as the programme grows, and the date above shows the latest version.

Your details

The details you give must be accurate. This applies to every form: applications, whitelist requests, service requests and contact messages.

Tasks and verification

Scouters complete data tasks, and a task counts once its submission is verified. Submit accurate, clean entries and meet batch deadlines.

We can remove anyone who submits false or copied data.

Points, ranks and rewards

Points, ranks and rewards depend on verified task submissions and can change as the programme grows.

  • Scouter track: Scouter, Veteran, Master, Super Scouter. 100 verified tasks (Super Scouter) unlocks the Commander role.
  • Commander track: Commander, Veteran, Master, Super Commander. 100 verified tasks (Super Commander) unlocks core employment opportunities.
  • 25 verified tasks on either track lets you mint that track’s NFT Badge.

Commanders and referral links

There are only 30 Commander spots. Ruptvate decides who is activated as a Commander. A Commander’s referral link lets them invite Scouters into their squad, and Commanders earn point overrides on verified work completed by their squad.

NFT badges

Ruptvate NFT badges, including the Early Supporters Badge, are membership badges. They are not investments and no profit is promised.

The Early Supporters Badge is limited to the first 1,000 supporters and is coming soon. Applying for the whitelist does not guarantee a place. Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone, including us.

Services

Consulting, Digital Campus and Marketplace requests are enquiries. Sending one does not guarantee a service, placement or listing.

Acceptable use

Do not submit false or copied data, impersonate others, abuse referral links, or try to disrupt the site or the programme.

Questions

Email support@ruptvate.com or use the contact form.

What we collect

We collect what the forms ask for, depending on the form:

  • Contact and service requests: name, email, organisation and your message.
  • Whitelist: wallet address and email.
  • Commander application: name, email, Telegram, X and LinkedIn details, country, state and city, education and experience, your reason for applying, and your CV.
  • Scouter sign-up: name, email, Telegram and X details, country, and the referral code you used.

How we use it

We use it to review applications, run the programme, answer you and contact you about your request.

What is public

  • If you become a Commander, your X handle and picture show on your referral page.
  • We do not publish your CV.
  • Wallet addresses are public on a blockchain by nature. Only share your public address, never your seed phrase or private key.

Your choices

To ask for your data to be deleted, email support@ruptvate.com.

Cookies and third parties

This site does not set its own cookies and has no advertising trackers. Fonts load from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg, so those providers see a normal web request from your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ranks, Commanders, Scouters, badges and services.

The network

What is Ruptvate?
Ruptvate is a task and squad network. Commanders lead squads of Scouters, Scouters complete data tasks, and every verified task counts towards rank. See How it works.
How is rank earned?
Rank is earned by verified tasks, nothing else. Each track climbs through Veteran and Master to a Super rank at 100 verified tasks.
What counts as a verified task?
A task counts once its submission has been checked and verified. Accurate, clean entries delivered on deadline are what move you up. False or copied data can get you removed.

Commanders

How many Commander spots are there?
Only 30. Apply early to lock in your referral link.
What does a Commander do?
Commanders claim a referral link, invite Scouters to build a squad, oversee task submission quality and keep the squad active.
What do Commanders earn?
Point overrides on all verified work completed by their squad. At 25 verified squad tasks they can mint the Commander NFT Badge, and at 100 tasks (Super Commander) core employment opportunities unlock.
Who gets activated as a Commander?
Ruptvate decides who is activated. Apply here and we review every application.

Scouters

What does a Scouter do?
Scouters complete assigned data-collection tasks accurately, submit clean entries and hit batch deadlines, working with their Commander to lift the squad.
What do Scouters earn?
Points for every verified task. At 25 verified tasks you can mint the Scouter NFT Badge, and at 100 tasks (Super Scouter) the Commander role unlocks.
How do I join?
Join as a Scouter. If a Commander invited you, use their referral link so you land in their squad.

Badge

What is the Early Supporters Badge?
A limited membership badge for the first 1,000 supporters, priced at $100. It is coming soon, and you can apply for the whitelist on the badge page.
Is the badge an investment?
No. Ruptvate NFT badges are membership badges, not investments, and no profit is promised.
What do you need from my wallet?
Only your public wallet address. Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.

Services and support

What services does Ruptvate offer?
Consulting, Digital Campus and Marketplace. Consulting covers advisory, process optimization, emerging technology training, workforce development and digital transformation. See Services.
How do I get help?
Email support@ruptvate.com or use the contact form. An AI Support Agent is coming soon.
How do I delete my data?
Email support@ruptvate.com. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
